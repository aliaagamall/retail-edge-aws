# Business Requirements

## 1. Business Context

RetailEdge Inc. is a mid-size e-commerce company currently operating its platform on three bare-metal servers in a co-location data center.

The company plans to migrate its platform to AWS before the current co-location contract expires in 90 days.

## 2. Business Objectives

The AWS migration should:

- Improve platform availability during seasonal traffic peaks, particularly Black Friday.
- Reduce revenue loss caused by production downtime.
- Support the platform's current user base and peak traffic requirements.
- Reduce operational overhead associated with the current co-location environment.
- Reduce the time and risk associated with production deployments.
- Minimize human error during deployment operations.
- Provide a scalable foundation for future growth.

## 3. Business Impact

The current platform experiences approximately 2–3 hours of downtime every Black Friday, resulting in an estimated loss of $80,000 in sales.

Production deployments currently require approximately four hours of manual work, and three production incidents occurred during the current quarter due to human error during deployments.

## 4. Migration Deadline

The migration must be completed within 90 days to align with the renewal date of the existing co-location contract.
